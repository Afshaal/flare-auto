# Flare Auto v1.2.2

More realistic offline arrival times.

- Off peak: 50 km/h on Canal Road, 80 km/h on Ring Road and motorways, 40 km/h on other roads.
- Peak hours (07:30–09:30 and 16:30–19:30, Monday–Friday by default): estimates are multiplied by 1.8 for trips
  over 6 km and by 1.2 for shorter trips.
- Peak hours are on by default; change the windows and days, or switch them off, in
  **Settings › Offline maps › Peak hours and speeds**. Estimates only; routes are not changed.

## Map packs in the app

- **Settings › Offline maps › Download map pack** lists the packs linked from the README on GitHub, downloads
  the one you choose and installs it after checking every file.
- Interrupted downloads continue where they stopped.

Installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Map packs are on the v1.2.1 release.
