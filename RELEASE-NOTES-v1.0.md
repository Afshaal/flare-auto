# Flare Auto v1.0

Initial release of the customizable offline landscape launcher. Includes a configurable home screen, media card, app drawer, dock, themes, wallpapers, screensaver and startup options.

Tested by the project owner on an Android 9 Civic head unit. Other devices and vendor-specific shortcuts may behave differently.

Known issue: after switching to the head unit's Bluetooth source during CarPlay playback, the media card may lose artwork. Reconnecting DiPlay has restored it on the tested unit.

Grant notification-listener access for supported media metadata and controls.
